## Who operates Chai Khapa

Chai Khapa is an independent app for recording and settling small group expenses. For privacy questions, corrections, or account-deletion requests, email **SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE**. Replace this placeholder with a real, monitored support address before publishing this page.

## What information we collect

When you sign in with Google, our authentication provider may receive your Google account identifier, email address, name, profile picture (if supplied), and email-verification status. We use this information to authenticate you and create a recognizable Chai Khapa profile. We do not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts, or calendar. You may choose to enter or update your first and last name in the app.

## Group and expense information

As group features become available, the app records groups you create or join, group membership, invitation activity, the dates and amounts of recorded sessions, who paid, the people selected as attendees, each participant’s calculated share, and corrections to those records. When settlement features are introduced, the app may also store settlement amounts, payment-status claims, and recipient confirmations. These records exist to calculate balances and provide a shared history of group expenses.

## Notifications and optional payment details

As notifications become available, we may record notification text or event references, recipients, timestamps, and read status. If you later choose to add a Raast ID or mobile-wallet number through an enabled payment-details feature, that information will be optional and used to help the people who owe you money find your preferred transfer method. We will publish updated disclosures and apply appropriate access controls before launching collection of payment identifiers. Chai Khapa does not itself move money between users.

## How we use your information

We use information to sign you in, maintain your account, organize groups, calculate and display expense shares, keep an auditable transaction history, deliver app notifications when those features are available, secure the service, detect errors or misuse, and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal information or use your Google profile data for personalized advertising.

## Who can see your information

Other members of a group may see your display name, relevant participation, payments recorded for the group, and shares or balances needed for group accounting. Only members authorized for a group should have access to its financial records. Public visitors should not be able to read private group data. If the app offers a shareable WhatsApp summary in the future, it will be initiated by the user; the person sharing it is responsible for checking what the message contains.

## Service providers and international processing

Google provides sign-in; Supabase provides authentication and application database services; and Cloudflare hosts the website and may process operational request information. These providers may process information in countries outside Pakistan, according to their services and policies. We may use additional providers for optional push or email delivery when those channels are enabled, and will update this notice as appropriate. We do not give these providers permission to sell your information on our behalf.

## Browser storage and technical data

We use browser storage to maintain your sign-in session and basic application state. Our hosting and authentication providers may receive technical information, such as device or browser type, IP address, request timestamps, and diagnostics, to deliver and protect the service. We do not currently describe any third-party advertising or cross-site tracking as part of Chai Khapa.

## Security

We use HTTPS, service-provider security features, and database authorization controls appropriate to the features in operation. No internet service can promise absolute security. Do not enter passwords, payment card details, or confidential banking credentials into expense descriptions or other free-text fields. Sensitive optional payment identifiers will not be enabled until the planned access controls and protection measures have been implemented and tested.

## Retention, corrections, and deletion

We keep account and group-expense records while needed to operate Chai Khapa, account for unsettled obligations, resolve disputes, prevent misuse, or meet applicable legal requirements. A request to delete an account may require us to retain limited historical transaction records connected to other group members while removing or reducing personal identifiers where feasible. Contact **SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE** to request access, correction, or deletion. We will review requests subject to applicable law and the rights of other group members.

## Children

Chai Khapa is designed for colleagues managing shared expenses and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us using the support address above.

## Updates to this policy

We may revise this policy when the application changes—for example, when push notifications, email summaries, or optional payment identifiers are introduced. The updated policy will show a new effective date on this page. Material changes will be communicated in the app where practical.
